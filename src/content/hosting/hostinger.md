Hostinger is the budget pick. If you need a small business site up this week for the price of a couple of coffees a month, this is the box to tick.

## Why it made the list

LiteSpeed + built in caching means a basic WordPress site on Hostinger feels faster than it should at the price. The hPanel dashboard is the first shared-host control panel I actually like handing to a non technical client.

## Real world numbers

On the Premium plan with LiteSpeed Cache, a lean WordPress site lands around 250ms Time-to-First-Byte in the US and under 2s Largest Contentful Paint. Solid for the money.

## The honest tradeoff

The $2.49/mo price is only for long multi year commitments. Month to month is closer to $11. Not a dealbreaker, just know what you are signing up for.

## Verdict

If budget is the top constraint and you only need one or two small business sites, Hostinger gives the best speed to dollar ratio in the shared hosting tier. [See current plans](https://hostinger.com?REFERRALCODE=REPLACE_ME).
