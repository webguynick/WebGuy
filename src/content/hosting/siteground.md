SiteGround is the host I recommend to clients who run a serious WordPress site, want page caching handled for them, and actually need a human on chat at 2am when something breaks.

## Why it is on my short list

SiteGround's custom Nginx + PHP setup delivers WordPress pages noticeably faster than cPanel hosts at the same price tier. Their SG Optimizer plugin handles caching, image optimization, and CSS/JS combining without the usual plugin roulette.

## Real world numbers

On a mid-range SiteGround plan, a fresh WordPress install with SG Optimizer hits Time-to-First-Byte around 180ms and Largest Contentful Paint under 1.8s in most US regions. That is competitive with managed WordPress hosts charging three times the price.

## The honest tradeoff

Intro pricing is genuinely good. Renewal pricing lands closer to $15/month on the entry plan, so budget for that at year two. You can still leave at any time.

## Verdict

For a small business running WordPress who wants "it just works" without DIY performance tuning, SiteGround is the pick. [Check current pricing](https://www.siteground.com/index.htm?afcode=REPLACE_ME).
